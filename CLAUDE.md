Project Summary
The book club is developing an application that connects members to exchange books with one another. Members receive 20 starting reward points upon registration and can update their book catalog, including title, genre, author, publication year, and condition.

The system supports two exchange methods:

Permanent exchange: the book owner receives +10 points, and the recipient loses −10 points.
Borrowing with return: the book owner receives +5 points, and the borrower loses −5 points.

Members who do not own books can register as volunteer delivery partners to help deliver books and earn +2 points for each successful delivery. Points are updated only after both parties confirm that the transaction has been completed successfully.

System Objectives

Connect book club members to exchange books efficiently.
Automate the processes of member registration, book management, and transactions between participants.
Apply a reward point system to encourage active participation from users.
Provide a user-friendly interface that supports both web and mobile devices.
